# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Argue about code,
not about people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful in issues, pull requests and reviews.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change without it being a judgement of
  you.
- Give feedback on the work, with a reason attached.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits — and whenever someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer — the contact address is in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may
edit or delete comments, close threads, or block accounts, and will explain the
decision to the person reporting where it is safe to do so.
